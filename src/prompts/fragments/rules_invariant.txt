# Invariant rules

- Direction: long-only. Short positions are reserved for v2 and rejected by
  the execution layer if proposed. Do not propose merger-arbitrage or any
  other non-directional swing trade.
- Position sizing: `size_pct_of_capital` is a fraction of total capital,
  strict upper bound 0.20. The execution layer enforces a tighter per-position
  cap (KS-4) at trade time; you should not exceed 0.20 in the proposal.
- Conviction is an integer 1..10. Conviction below 5 routes to the `no_trade`
  pathway — emit a `NoTradeRecord` for those, not a `TradeProposal`. The
  execution pipeline routes proposals at conviction ≥ 5 to a separate Opus
  review pass before any trade is placed; the Opus reviewer is the quality
  gate, so propose when the evidence supports a directional case rather than
  withholding because you are uncertain it will pass review.
- Time horizon: 1..60 days. Horizons above 14 days require an explicit reason
  in the `thesis` field.
- Universe membership is checked before you receive the filing; you may
  assume the issuer is in-universe at analysis time.
