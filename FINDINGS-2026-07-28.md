# Capital Allocation Findings — 2026-07-28

## Executive Summary

Quinn is **missing high-conviction opportunities** due to capital constraints at market open. On 2026-07-09, we identified FBRX (a +400% winner) but couldn't trade it due to KS-7 cash reserve blocking — all capital was locked in stale/underperforming positions.

Two key issues identified:
1. **Trailing stops too loose** → small winners bleed into losers
2. **No systematic capital rotation** → can't take 2 new positions at market open

---

## Issue 1: Trailing Stops — Small Winners Becoming Bleeders

### The Problem

Current trail system (15% base width with staged tightening at 20%/35% gains) is **giving back too much profit on small/medium winners**.

### Evidence

**ARCT (execution 11):**
- Entry: $7.23
- Peak: $8.19 (+12% = $71 profit)
- Trail engaged at +12%, but 15% trail width placed stop at $6.96 (BELOW entry)
- Exit: loss instead of small win
- **Trail too wide for the gain magnitude**

**MGTX:**
- Peak profit: +$76
- Final exit: -$6
- **Gave back $82 from peak**

**Contrast with FEIM (execution 37):**
- Entry: $51.03
- Peak: $79.85 (+56%)
- Exit: $75.86 (+48.7%)
- Trail system worked perfectly: 5% staged trail engaged, only gave back $3.99 (5% from peak)
- **This is the target behavior**

### Root Cause

The 15% base trail width is appropriate for BIG runners (30%+ gains) but **too loose for 10-20% moves**. Small winners hit the trail activation threshold (1.0R ~= 10% gain) but the 15% trail immediately puts the stop below/at entry, erasing the gain.

### Potential Solutions

- Tighten base trail width (15% → 10% or 12%)
- Lower the first `trail_stages` threshold (currently 20% → maybe 12% or 15%)
- Adjust trail activation threshold relative to trail width
- **Let the other Claude decide the approach**

## Issue 2: Capital Rotation — Can't Take Fresh Signals

### The Problem

Quinn stays **fully deployed** (7-11 positions, $200-280k) by design, but this creates a **liquidity trap**: when a high-conviction signal arrives at market open, there's no cash available to trade it.

### Evidence

**FBRX incident (2026-07-09):**
- Quinn found FBRX at market open, conviction 6, sizing $280
- **Proposal REJECTED** by KS-7 cash reserve
- FBRX went +400% (bought 1 share manually at $21.25 for tracking)
- Meanwhile, capital was locked in:
  - SOTK: 20 days held, underperforming
  - RNA: 13 days held, underperforming

**This is the REAL problem** — not the kill-switch rule (KS-7 worked as intended), but the lack of systematic rotation to ensure capacity.

### Current Behavior

Quinn is designed to stay fully invested:
```sql
-- Typical deployment snapshot
SELECT COUNT(*) as positions, SUM(market_value) as deployed
FROM positions WHERE qty != 0;
-- Result: 7-11 positions, $200-280k deployed (on ~$280k total equity)
```

**This is correct** — we WANT to stay fully deployed for maximum opportunity capture. But we need **pre-market rotation** to ensure fresh capital availability.

### Analysis: Holding Period vs Performance

Initial holding-period analysis was **WRONG** because it measured exit timing, not peak timing. Re-analysis showed:

- **Peaks happen early** (often 3-7 days)
- **Exits happen later** (after positions give back gains)
- Positions held 8-14 days often in negative territory (the "dead zone")
- Positions held 15+ days are often stale with no catalyst

**The pattern:**
1. Day 1-7: Position runs, hits peak
2. Day 8-14: Gives back gains, trail doesn't tighten enough
3. Day 15+: Dead money, no new catalyst, waiting for stop

### User Requirement

> **"We would like the system to, in any way it likes, give us the ability to have at LEAST two new positions at market open. No requirement on morning to use them, but at least the ABILITY to."**

### Goal

Ensure Quinn can ALWAYS take **2 new high-conviction positions** at market open by **systematically freeing capital** before market close.

This does NOT mean:
- Force 2 exits every day
- Always use the freed capital
- Change position sizing or KS-7 logic

This DOES mean:
- Identify weakest/stalest positions before close
- Cut the 2 weakest IF needed to ensure capacity
- Criteria: unarmed positions (trail not engaged), held 7+ days, <5% gain, or in the dead zone

### Proposed Solution

**Daily pre-market-close capital rotation sweep:**

1. Run at **3:30 PM ET** (30 min before close)
2. Check current position count vs `ks5_max_concurrent`
3. If >= (max - 2), identify candidates to cut:
   - Unarmed positions (trail not engaged)
   - Held ≥7 days with unrealized gain <5%
   - OR held 8-14 days with negative returns (dead zone)
4. Rank by weakness (longest-held + lowest gain)
5. Close the 2 weakest via thesis_coordinator

**Implementation:**
- New module: `src/jobs/capital_rotation.py`
- Systemd timer: `quinn-capital-rotation.timer` at 15:30 ET weekdays
- Uses existing `thesis_coordinator.close()` mechanism
- Full audit trail in journal + logs

**Key design principle:**
- Let Quinn decide HOW to ensure 2-slot capacity
- Operator requirement: **guarantee the ABILITY to take 2 fresh positions**
- No prescription on which positions to cut or when to use the freed slots
